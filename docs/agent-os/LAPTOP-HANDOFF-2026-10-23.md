## The product we are building

VYREN is a persistent social-media creative organization for a brand, starting with a solo founder or creator. Instagram and X come first. It should research and track competitors, plan campaigns, write posts, generate images and reels (Higgsfield is the selected media-provider candidate), learn from approved guidance and measured outcomes, and eventually work through governed virtual-computer sessions where appropriate. Every public post requires approval of its exact account, text, asset, and timing. The persistent bot is an identity with its own role, memory, skills, tools, capabilities, and runtime; a model is a routed component, while a separate governed executor performs actions. The product should own its decision state and taste history without claiming it can dispense with models or that early preference updates constitute reinforcement learning.

The architecture begins with **one reliable Content Producer**, then expands to campaign-scoped teams. A brand can define reusable roles and approved guidance, but each campaign gets its **own manager bot and bot instances**, separate chats, tasks, context, campaign memory, and eventually computer sessions. The manager assigns bounded work and reports approval requests. It may propose a new role but needs Subham's permission to create or activate it. It cannot change campaign goals, publish, spend, grant new capabilities, or promote durable guidance without the relevant human decision. Switching campaigns must not mix their state; authorized bounded work can continue in the background. See [confirmed campaign intent](../intent/campaign-workspaces-and-bot-teams.md), [social-media thesis](VYREN-SOCIAL-MEDIA-THESIS-AND-ROADMAP-v0.1.md), and the [campaign capability map](../../CAPABILITY-MAP-campaign-workspaces.md).

## Research versus VYREN decisions

The [Grok evidence correction](GROK-BOT-PRIMITIVES-EVIDENCE-CORRECTION.md) links public xAI documentation for persistent role bots, direct/group messaging, a shared workspace, and separate bot screens on one shared computer. It does **not** prove Grok's private storage schema, scheduler, or model router. VYREN's campaign isolation, separate action service, model routing, and stricter per-post approval are VYREN design choices.
